# For mystery shoppers

## What is mystery shopping
Mystery shopping means visiting a store, restaurant, bank branch or other business as an ordinary customer, noticing how the service, cleanliness, product availability and processes are, and then submitting an honest report. The business learns how real customers are treated. No special experience is needed for most assignments, though some campaigns prefer experience in a category such as food and beverage or retail.

## What do I do on a mystery shopping visit
Visit the shop within the visit window, behave like a normal customer, observe the things the form asks about (for example greeting, wait time, cleanliness, staff knowledge, product availability) and then fill in the report honestly. Stay discreet and never reveal you are a mystery shopper at the location. Never guess or invent observations, and never complete a form without actually visiting. The assistant can explain what a form question means but will not suggest answers.

## How do I get invited to an assignment
When a campaign has shops that need visits, the platform recommends the best-matching shoppers for each shop and ISN emails them an invitation. The email has a button (VIEW ASSIGNMENT) with your personal secure link. The link opens a page showing the shop, its location, the visit window, the category and the compensation, with Accept and Decline buttons.

## How are shoppers matched to shops
Each shopper gets a match score out of 100 for a shop from seven factors: how well their profile fits the requirement (30 points), distance from the shop (20 points, anything beyond about 60 km scores zero), experience in the shop's category (15), current availability (15), completion history (10), rating (5) and past work for the same client (5). Scores of 90 and above are Top matches, 80 to 89 Strong, 70 to 79 Potential and below 70 Low. Keeping your availability, location and profile current and completing assignments you accept raises your chances.

## How do I accept or decline an invitation
Open the personal link from your invitation email and press Accept or Decline. You can also tell the chat assistant on that page. A response can be recorded only once per invitation. Accepting records your interest and the ISN coordinator or the source platform confirms the final assignment, so a shop is only called "scheduled" when it really is.

## What happens after I accept
Your acceptance is recorded against your invitation and shows up for ISN in real time. The final booking and any instructions come from the coordinator or the source mystery shopping platform. The assistant can record your preferred date or shop interest, set a reminder, and explain the instructions, but it never books a shop on the source platform by itself.

## Is the invitation optional
Yes. Assignments are always optional. You choose whether to accept each invitation. Each assignment has a visit window with a start and end date during which the visit has to be done.

## How much will I be paid and when
Shoppers are paid per completed assignment. The exact compensation for an assignment is shown on its invitation page and in the assignment details the assistant can look up. Some shops also carry a bonus, an extra amount on top of the standard pay that the client funds and pays directly to the shopper who completes it. Payment dates and payout timing are not stored in this system, so the assistant cannot give a payout date; it will tell you what the invitation says and what to check next.

## What is a shop bonus
A bonus is an optional extra incentive the client sets for a specific shop that is hard to fill. It is on top of the normal compensation, funded and paid by the client outside ShopperMatch. The shopper who completes that shop receives it, and the invitation email mentions it when one is set.

## Why did I get several invitations
Each invitation is for one specific shop in one campaign. If several shops near you match your profile you can receive more than one. You can accept the ones you want and decline the rest. ISN's automatic recruiting (AI Autopilot) also limits how many unanswered invitations one shopper can hold at once (5 by default), so nobody is buried under invitations.

## How do I stop getting messages
Tell the assistant you want to stop being contacted. It records an opt-out immediately, and the email outbox, the voice-call follow-ups and the reminder system all respect it. If you also want your data deleted, say so and the request is passed on with the reason data deletion, because deleting data is not something the assistant can do itself.

## Will I get phone calls
If you do not reply to the emails, ISN may follow up with an automated AI voice call asking whether you are still interested. The call says at the start that it is automated and that it may be recorded. You can ask not to be called.

## What does the tracking in the email do
The email contains a tiny invisible image that shows ISN whether it was opened, and the button link is tracked when clicked. When you load the assignment page and press Accept or Decline that is recorded against your own invitation only. This is how ISN knows which invited shopper responded. Your link is unique to you; do not forward it.

## Is my personal data safe
Links use random unguessable tokens, internal ids and raw IP addresses are never exposed or stored, client brands only see counts rather than your name or contact details, and you can opt out of messages at any time. The assistant never asks for passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar, PAN or bank details.

## What can the shopper chat assistant help with
Opened from your invitation link, the assistant (named Maya by default) knows your campaign. It can explain the campaign, check your eligibility against the requirements, list the shops that are available with fees and visit windows, explain form instructions, record your interest and preferred date, set an email reminder, handle a reschedule or cancel request and stop messages. Before it records anything it repeats the details and waits for your yes.
